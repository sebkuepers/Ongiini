Similarity · exploratory

Which systems write alike?

Pairwise chrF++ between system outputs (development split), ordered by clustering. "Shared errors" restricts to items both systems got wrong (chrF++ < 30 vs the reference) and subtracts what their closeness to the reference alone predicts — a hint at shared sources or strategies, not evidence about training data.
