FAQ

What is Ongiini?

A free AI assistant you can chat with on WhatsApp. The name "Ongiini" means "How are you?" in Oshiwambo. It's built for people in Namibia.

Is it free?

Yes. Ongiini itself is free to use. Standard WhatsApp data charges from your mobile provider may apply.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Afrikaans for now. Oshiwambo and other Namibian languages are on the roadmap as language support improves.

What can I ask?

Anything — weather, news, current prices, exchange rates, general questions, help drafting a message, ideas, advice. Ongiini can search the web when it needs up-to-date information.

Who can use it?

Anyone with a Namibian (+264) WhatsApp number. We're starting small and may expand to other countries later.
