How it works

Three steps, then you're learning.

Step 1

Tell us your goal

A few quick questions — your level, what you're trying to do. In your own words.

Step 2

AI builds your curriculum

The model writes a plan around your goal. Modules, topics, lessons before drills.

Step 3

Learn by chatting

Lessons and exercises appear in the conversation. Ask questions any time — the coach answers and brings you back.

This is a prototype. Starting with Afrikaans because that's what most users tell us they want first. The same engine can teach a language, prep you for an interview, or drill a complicated university subject through targeted repetition. More tracks are next.
