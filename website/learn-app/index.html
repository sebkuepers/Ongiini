How it works

Three steps, then you're learning.

Step 1

Tell us your goal

A few quick questions — your level, what you're trying to do. In your own words.

Intake
"What do you actually want to be able to do in Afrikaans?"
A job interview at SPAR in two weeks.
Step 2

AI builds your curriculum

The model writes a plan around your goal — what to teach first, where you need depth, how to keep it efficient.

Your plan
  • Greetings & introductions
  • Describing your experience
  • Common interview questions
  • Asking your own questions
Step 3

Learn with smart cards

Vocab, translation, free-form production. Type your answer; the model grades it and tells you what's off. Stuck cards come back sooner.

Vocab · box 1
How do you say "thank you very much" in Afrikaans?
Correct — "baie dankie". Now in box 2.

This is a prototype. Starting with Afrikaans because that's what most users tell us they want first. The same engine can teach a language, prep you for an interview, or drill a complicated university subject through targeted repetition. More tracks — and richer integration with what we know about you across Ongiini — are next.
